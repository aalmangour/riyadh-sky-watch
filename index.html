What to do

911 Unified emergency998 Civil Defense997 Ambulance
During an alert or after an explosion
  1. Move away from windows, balconies and rooftops into an interior room, ideally on a low floor.
  2. If you are outside, get into the nearest building or behind a solid barrier.
  3. Stay there until the Civil Defense all-clear. Interception debris can fall for several minutes.
  4. Do not approach debris or impact sites, do not gather, and do not film: the Ministry of Interior has warned that filming interceptions or debris sites is an offence.
Make sure official alerts reach your phone
  • iPhone: Settings → Notifications → scroll to the bottom → turn on Government / Emergency Alerts.
  • Android: Settings → Safety & emergency → Wireless emergency alerts → on.
  • The National Early Warning Platform (Civil Defense) sends a loud alert to every phone in the threatened area, and an all-clear afterwards.
